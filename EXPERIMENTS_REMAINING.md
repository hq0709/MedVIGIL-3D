## Blocking — do not submit the 3D section without these

### R1. The distance sub-task under richer input  ·  ~20 min
**Why.** §3 of the section shows models cannot read a distance off three
annotated planes. The one honest rebuttal is that three planes do not contain
it. §5 answers this only for the *composite* question, which §4 shows collapses
even with the distance supplied in text — so the downstream failure masks the
metric channel. The clean control is the distance question itself, asked on the
richer renderings.

**What is needed.** `spatialgen/run_subtasks.py` takes `--condition` over the
four identification conditions only. Extend it to accept the input-richness arms
(`slices9`, `axial25`, `zoom`) by routing through
`run_input_richness.render_arm`, then:

```bash
for ARM in slices3 axial25 zoom; do
  for M in qwen32b internvl; do
    python spatialgen/run_subtasks.py --subtask distance --condition $ARM \
      --model $M --n 300 --device cuda:0 \
      --out results_new/id_common_${M}_subtask-distance-${ARM}.jsonl
  done
done
```

**Reading it.** Still at 25% with 25 contiguous slices and with the structures
magnified → the metric channel is absent, not starved, and §3 stands as the
core result. Above chance → the finding becomes "three slices is too little",
which is a different and weaker paper; §3 must be rewritten.

### R2. Native volumetric models on one library stack  ·  ~15 min, 6 jobs
**Why.** The committed response-channel numbers do not reproduce: 42.9% today
against 76.4% recorded, same 20 volumes, 63.6% per-item agreement, same script
under a different stack. Until this is resolved the paper cannot quote any
framing-repair effect for M3D-LaMed or Med3DVLM, and the claim that four models
are unmeasurable rests on files that do not regenerate.

```bash
python runs/make_program.py                    # writes runs/jobs_e6.jsonl
python runs/run_queue.py --jobs runs/jobs_e6.jsonl --gpus 6,7 \
    --share-headroom 50 --skip-existing
```

`sanity_controls.py --framing {native,montage}` measures both framings in one
run, which the committed files never did — they were produced by editing the
constant, so the two framings were never compared within a stack.

## Worth doing, not blocking

### R6. Metric floor test on synthetic images  ·  ~15 min · **not in the original programme**
Two markers a known distance apart on a synthetic image, with the same 10 mm
bar and the same four-option format. If models are at chance there too, §3
generalises past CT and past medicine — a much larger claim for minutes of
compute. If they are not, §3 localises to medical imaging, which is also worth
knowing. Needs a small generator; nothing else in the repo does this.

### R7. Four-arm grounding with recorded fill  ·  ~1.5 h, 64 jobs
`analyse_roi_4arm.py` refuses to emit a pooled figure because the committed arms
mix `air`, `local` and unrecorded fills. That refusal is correct behaviour, and
the per-fill supplement table is survivable. `run_roi_arms.py` already records
`fill` per item, so one consistent campaign at equal n is all that is needed.

```bash
python runs/run_queue.py --jobs runs/jobs_e8.jsonl --gpus 6,7 \
    --share-headroom 50 --skip-existing
```

### R8. Reader study  ·  human time, no compute
`reader_study/` now holds 104 probes, 52 yes / 52 no, all five severity tiers,
with images generated on the corrected renderer and byte-identical to the
`identified` arm — so a reader-versus-model comparison is finally a comparison of
reasoning rather than of image geometry. Every `reader_answer` cell is empty.

Design points that were agreed and are worth keeping: **at least three readers**
so inter-reader agreement can be reported (Fleiss' κ or Gwet's AC1), and use the
`clinical_relevance__1to5` column — a 4–5 rating answers "are these probes
clinically meaningful?" directly, and a 1–2 rating is something to know before
submission rather than after.

Do not run this before R1: if the metric result changes, the input the readers
should be shown may change with it.

## Operational notes

- **GPU 6 is shared.** Another user's `fnd.model` job comes and goes and has
  peaked at 47 GB. `--share-headroom 50` computes our budget from what they
  currently hold plus a promised headroom, so their run is not killed. Do not
  schedule against free memory on that card.
- **Both cards for the 72B.** 146.8 GB sharded leaves no room for anything else.
  `runs/e4_watcher.sh` waits for a card with no co-tenant, sets
  `runs/.exclusive.pause` (which the queues honour), drains, runs, and releases.
- **The render cache is 2.7 GB and regenerable** (`runs/prerender.py`, ~10 min
  on 40 workers). It is gitignored. Purge it after any change to the renderer —
  a stale cache silently feeds the old images to every job.
- **Anything touching the renderer must re-run `run_identification_control.py
  selftest`.** The check it replaced — comparing the `plain` arm's accuracy with
  the published number — cannot fail, because both are at chance whether the
  renderer is right or wrong.
